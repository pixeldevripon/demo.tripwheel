Spec notes (proposal v0.1)

1
Traveler login implements master 6.4 verbatim: email plus booking reference, no passwords, no sign-up, rate-limited. Takeover chrome (checkout family): no nav, no distractions. Spec 2.
2
WhatsApp is the human fallback on every surface ("Customer support" is a banned phrase). Lockout copy routes here too. Spec 1.7, 2.3.
3
Form mechanics bundle: labels above fields, autocomplete attributes, inputmode, paste allowed. Error is generic on purpose: no account enumeration, identical message, status, and timing. Try a submit to see it; second try logs in (demo). Spec 1.1, 1.3, 2.4.
4
Lost-reference recovery mirrors the airline and Expedia convention. The response is always positive ("if that email has bookings...") so the flow leaks nothing. Spec 2.3.
5
Wrong-door routing: one quiet cross-link each way between traveler and operator surfaces, separate URLs per OTA convention. Admin is linked from nowhere. Spec 5.
6
Operator portal on operators.island.tours: per-person seats, roles, owner-gated payouts. Email plus password (NIST rules: no composition rules, no rotation, paste and managers allowed) then mandatory 2FA. Spec 3.1, 3.2.
7
2FA v1: authenticator app plus backup codes, white-glove enrolled at onboarding. WhatsApp code fallback is v1.1 (Meta template approval; shown here as demo). No SMS anywhere (NIST restricted channel, pumping fraud, island deliverability), no email codes. Codes: 5 attempts max, WhatsApp codes valid 10 minutes. Enrollment screens (QR, manual key, backup codes) are specced in 3.5. Demo: submit credentials, then any 6 digits verify. Spec 3.2.
8
Daily-use friction solved with device trust (30 days) plus a 14-day rolling session, never with fewer factors. FareHarbor and Booking.com pattern. Spec 3.2.5.
9
Anti-phishing line, adapted from the 6.5 email anti-fraud line: operators learn the same sentence travelers get. Pinned on the brand panel and repeated in the portal footer. The Booking.com partner-phishing economy is the cautionary tale. Spec 3.2.8.
10
Staff: Google Workspace SSO only, no app-level passwords. MFA and passkeys enforced once, at the IdP. hd claim plus allowlist verified server-side. Demo: first click shows the denied state, second logs in. Spec 4.1.
11
The denied message may be specific: identity is already verified by Google at this point, so no enumeration risk. Spec 4.2.
12
Obscurity is a courtesy, not a control: the subdomain isolates cookies and CSP, authorization lives in RLS and the allowlist. Every login and action writes an audit line. Spec 4.1, 1.6.
