Card carousels · one signifier rule for every card

S4j dev handoff · 2026-08-06 · register-honest demo card (Catamaran Day Trip, 3.5 shared component) · research: search-bar-benchmark.md sits next to this file, carousel sources in the footer

Note from Denley: how card photo carousels should behave

The rule. Desktop cards keep their photo carousel (5 to 7 photos plus a description slide, per 3.5), and the signifiers work in two volumes: at rest the dots are present but quiet (small, white at 60% with a soft shadow), on hover they go full white and the chevrons fade in. Chevrons are position-aware: photo 1 shows only a right arrow, the last slide only a left one. They also appear on keyboard focus, not just hover.

Why not the current version. Right now dots and chevrons only exist on hover, so at rest there is no signal at all that more photos exist, and tested UX research is unambiguous: without a visible cue users never discover the other photos, which makes the whole carousel dead weight. Always-on chevrons are the other extreme: eight cards in a grid all shouting arrows. Quiet dots at rest is the middle that works, and it is what Viator and Airbnb both converge on.

Mobile. Swipe is the interaction, never chevron-tapping, but the swipe must be visible: the same quiet dots stay on the photo at all times (today there is no signal at all, so nobody finds the other photos). No chevrons on mobile. Implementation stays native: CSS scroll-snap, no carousel library, images lazy-loaded, five slides max. Accessibility note for WCAG 2.5.1: the card link plus the full gallery on the tour page is the single-pointer path; keep it that way.

One spec consequence, decided. Swipeable galleries mean image-led mobile cards. That supersedes the older horizontal mobile card (image 40 / content 60) in 3.5: keep the image-led mobile cards you already built, now with the dots added (decision Denley, 2026-08-06, logged as DIT-20).

Scope: every card on the site. This is one rule for the one shared card component: All Tours, category pages, the destination Locals' favorites, search results, related tours, hub grids, and also the curated picks like "We've been on every boat" on the Klein Curaçao hub. Same dots, same chevrons, same behavior everywhere.

Behavior contract (copy-paste)

BEHAVIOR CONTRACT: every card image carousel, sitewide
1. Rest (desktop): dots present but quiet. 5px, white at 60% opacity with a soft shadow
   (stays readable on bright water photos). No chevrons at rest.
2. Hover, or keyboard focus on a control inside the card: dots go full white,
   chevrons fade in (120ms).
3. Chevrons are position-aware: no left chevron on photo 1, no right chevron on the
   last slide. That is why you sometimes only see a right arrow first: position, not styling.
4. The last slide is the description slide (3.5: 5 to 7 photos plus description).
5. Chevrons are real buttons with aria-labels (Previous photo / Next photo), tab-focusable,
   visible on focus. Dots are aria-hidden indicators, not controls. The card stays ONE link;
   chevrons and heart stopPropagation.
6. Mobile: swipe only, no chevrons, and the same quiet dots ALWAYS visible on the photo
   (that is the fix versus today: the swipe exists but nothing announces it).
   Native implementation: CSS scroll-snap, images lazy-loaded, 5 slides max.
   Image-led mobile cards are sanctioned (DIT-20, 2026-08-06); the older 3.5
   horizontal mobile card is superseded.
7. Reduced motion: slide changes are instant; scroll-snap stays (native, no animation JS).

Applies to every listing surface: All Tours, category pages, destination Locals' favorites,
search results, related tours, hub grids, and the curated picks (We've been on every boat)
on the Klein Curacao hub. One card component, one behavior.
Research behind the rule (retrieved 2026-08-06): Baymard, Always signpost hidden gallery images (users do not discover images without a visible cue; arrows are the clearest signal) · NN/g, Carousels on mobile (dots are weak signifiers; gestures need visible cues) · W3C, WCAG 2.5.1 Pointer Gestures, level A (swipe needs a single-pointer alternative) · Smashing Magazine, Carousel UX guidelines (dragging alone is not enough) · patterns: Airbnb (quiet dots always, arrows on hover), Viator desktop (dots always, position-aware arrows).
