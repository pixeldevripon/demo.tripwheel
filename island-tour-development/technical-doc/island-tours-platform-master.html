1. Platform and positioning

1.1 What Island Tours is

Island Tours is a Caribbean tour marketplace built on the "Built by Islanders" ethos: local curation as the ethical, locally owned alternative to global OTAs (Viator, GetYourGuide, Klook, Headout). Travelers discover and book tours and activities; local operators supply them; Island Tours earns a commission per booking.

Tagline: "Island Tours. Built by Islanders." The closing form "Built by Islanders." is used as a sign-off. The tagline stays in English in all seven locales: it is a brand mark, like a logo, never translated.

Positioning pillars

  1. Local curation, not an algorithmic catalog. Editorial picks are made by people who live on the islands.
  2. Ethical CRO. No fake urgency, no fake scarcity, no badge inflation, no dark patterns, no pre-checked add-ons. Paid placement is always labeled Sponsored.
  3. Transparency. Total price before checkout, no hidden fees, clear cancellation, claims always verifiable.
  4. Caribbean-proud voice. Warm, direct, first-person plural, never corporate.

1.4 Business model and money flow

Commission-based marketplace. Operators pay a tiered commission, locked at booking time as commission_amount on the booking record:

Tier (tier_key) Commission tier_rank
premium 30% 1
featured 27.5% 2
boosted 25% 3
organic 22.5% 4
standard 20% 5
Destination Spotlight 35% Separate placement block, never interleaved; max 3 simultaneous per destination; manual approval (operator requests, Island Tours approves)

standard is the default tier for new tours and the locked rate for operators on a negotiated 20% agreement; it deliberately ranks below organic, so a 20% operator who wants to outrank other base-rate tours moves up to organic at 22.5%. Tier mechanics are internal commercial logic, never user-facing. Tier also drives the deposit percentage (LD24): tour.deposit_pct ranges 20 to 30 in 2.5 steps. Revenue is recognized on tour completion; free cancellation window per tour (default 48h; a listing requirement, see 6.2).

Deposit and balance split. On the deposit models (operator_link, on_arrival) the traveler pays {X}% (deposit_pct) to Island Tours via Stripe at booking and the remaining balance is the operator's transaction; paid_in_full charges the full amount via Island Tours at booking; operator_full takes no payment at booking (5.8, C22). Four payment models govern the balance (canonical set, defined in the booking confirmation email spec; confirmed June 10, 2026):

Model Balance handling
operator_link (default) Operator emails a secure payment link; balance paid online before the deadline
on_arrival Balance paid in person on arrival (card or cash, or cash only, per tour)
paid_in_full Traveler paid 100% at booking via Island Tours
operator_full Operator collects the full amount; Island Tours took no deposit. Checkout takes no payment (5.8, C22)

The earlier LD24 statement "balance online, not cash on tour day" describes the operator_link default and is superseded as a platform-wide rule by this four-model set.

Two-phase operator visibility (platform principle). Before payment, all copy is agentless: the widget and modals say "You'll get a secure link to pay the rest" and never name or spotlight the operator (disintermediation control). After booking, the operator is named deliberately: on operator_link tours the Thank You page and the confirmation email say the operator will send the balance link, so that email is expected and not mistaken for phishing (the C2 mitigation). Pre-payment agentless, post-booking named. (Confirmed June 10, 2026.)

A destination-scoped registry maps every slug to exactly one page type (category, hub, collection, tour, or reserved). Rules:

  1. 19 categories plus the reserved tours slug are pre-seeded for every destination at creation: 20 protected slugs per destination.
  2. Slugs are locale-independent and unique per destination. The registry resolves the page type at request time; linking components never need to know the type (a quick link simply navigates to /{locale}/{destination}/{slug}/).
  3. Renames create a 301 entry in the redirect table automatically.
  4. Deleted slugs enter a 90-day soft-delete cooldown before they can be reused, protecting against stale external links and search-index confusion.

6.2 Payment and cancellation lifecycle confirmed June 10, 2026

The window rules:

  • tour.cancellation_hours: enum [24, 48, 72, 168], default 48. CMS blocks values outside the enum.
  • cancelDeadline = tour start minus cancellation_hours (computed in tour-local time, displayed with "(local time)").
  • Book: on the deposit models, deposit {X}% to Island Tours; paid_in_full charges the full amount; operator_full takes no payment (5.8). Booking confirmed instantly on every model. Two emails follow: the Island Tours confirmation and, on operator_link tours, the operator's balance email.
  • Up to the deadline: cancel for a full refund of any amount paid; on operator_link, pay the balance.
  • After the deadline: locked. Forfeiting is never automatic (the platform does not track operator_link balance payments in v1, so "unpaid" cannot be machine-determined): the operator reports non-payment, the admin confirms, and only that confirmation forfeits the deposit and releases the spot (conflict log 84).
  • Operator-forced cancellation (unsafe conditions): full refund or free reschedule, always.

This unified window supersedes the earlier split model (balance at 72h, cancellation at 48h) that survives in the widget brief §6 intro and §6.2 operator copy; those passages are rewritten per 6.5. Quality score (nightly job, read-only at query time, range 0 to 100): 

quality_score =
  (avg_rating / 5)               * 40 +
  (min(review_count, 100) / 100) * 25 +
  (listing_completeness / 100)   * 20 +
  (conversion_rate / max_conv)   * 15

max_conv is the highest conversion rate among active tours in the same category, recomputed per run. Same-tier collisions are expected and valid: tours sharing a tier order by quality score, then id; there is no per-category tier cap.

Bookability filter: a tour is excluded from every ranked result set, regardless of tier, when status != active, when is_bookable = false, or when it has no availability in the next 30 days. An excluded tour does not occupy a position (the next eligible tour moves up) and is not billed for its tier during the unbookable period.

7.3 Affiliate program confirmed June 10, 2026

  • Rate: 8% of the total tour price (GMV), funded entirely out of Island Tours' commission take. Worked example: $240 booking at 25% tier yields $60 commission; the affiliate earns $19.20, Island Tours nets $40.80.
  • Platform: Trackdesk (primary). Selected for server-side postback, dynamic commission amounts, an on-hold to approved lifecycle that maps to the cancellation window, the widest payout rails, and scriptless GDPR-compliant tracking. Tapfiliate is the mature alternative, FirstPromoter the middle option. 8.1 Principles

  1. Conversion value = commission_amount, never GMV: Smart Bidding learns from real margin.
  2. One booking_complete dataLayer event on the TYP feeds four GTM tags: Conversion Linker, Google Ads, GA4 (purchase), Meta Pixel. No per-tour or per-campaign tags.
  3. Enhanced Conversions and Advanced Matching on all available hashed PII (email, phone via libphonenumber-js normalization, name, address), hashed server-side.
  4. Server-side Meta CAPI fires in parallel with the browser Pixel, deduplicated by event id (iOS 14+ recovery).
  5. Server-side idempotency via conversion_fired_at on the bookings table: refreshes, email revisits, and shared links never double-fire. Never localStorage.
  6. Cancellation and refund adjustments flow back to Google Ads and Meta via API, which requires click id (gclid, gbraid, wbraid, fbclid) and UTM capture at booking creation (dev spec §14).
  7. Consent Mode v2 from scratch with regional defaults: EEA denied by default, US and CA granted. CMP selection (Cookiebot or Iubenda) precedes the GTM build.

8.2 Flow

/payment/processing (lean intermediate, webhook wait, zero tags) → Stripe webhook confirms, idempotently (8.1 item 5; webhook retries are safe; processed Stripe event ids live in a dedicated stripe_webhook_events table, dev spec §15) → redirect to /{destination}/thank-you/{bookingRef} → server component loads the booking, normalizes currency, hashes PII, and sets conversion_fired_at before render (mark-first: a client push that never executes is an accepted false negative, never a double fire) → client component pushes booking_complete once (production only, staging guard) → GTM fans out to the four tags → CAPI posts server-side with the shared event id. operator_full bookings bypass this flow: no charge and no webhook; the booking is created confirmed at commit and redirects straight to the TYP, where the mark-first idempotency and the 8.3 contract apply unchanged (5.8, conflict log 79).

dataLayer field Source Notes
booking_value bookings.commission_amount EUR, always; never booking_total_eur (8.1 item 1)

booking_ref bookings.display_ref Transaction id for dedupe across all platforms
tour_id, tour_name bookings.tour_id, tours.name GA4 item_id and item_name
