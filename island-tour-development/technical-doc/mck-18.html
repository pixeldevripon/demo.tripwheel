3 · Why the stored field still has to be cleaned

The founder decision changes what the hub page shows. It does not change what the database holds, and that is worth being explicit about, because the two look identical on screen and only one of them survives a CMS edit.

Composing and printing look the same. They fail differently.
  • With a clean field the hub page composes hub plus title in one rule. With the hub baked into the field it prints it raw, and every other surface needs its own stripping rule. Someone forgets one, which has now happened three times.
  • With a dirty field the tour page H1 has to strip “Klein Curaçao ” only to prepend “Klein Curaçao: ”, and the collection page needs a third stripping rule.
  • The fix is a data pass: strip each tour’s prefix using that tour’s own primary_hub value, never a blind find and replace on the words “Klein Curaçao”, or a tour whose real name contains them breaks. Then delete the stripping logic from the surfaces that compensate today.
  • At 24 tours this is a small pass. At 300 it is a migration. The v1.11 changelog ties the hub-free title to the operator-neutral register re-slugging, so it is register cleanup, not a display preference.

Open, and needing an answer before any of the proposed rows are built.
  • The subject line does not fit, in any shape. The locked subject is You’re booked: {tourName} on {dateShort}, and there are two more variants: Reservation sent: for operator_full, and a sub-24-hour “You’re booked for tomorrow” form. With the composed name, the middot reshape runs to 61 characters and a phone cuts it at roughly 35, so it reads “You’re booked · Klein Curaçao: Full” and the date never survives. Putting the date first, “12 Aug, 07:30 · Klein Curaçao: Full-Day Catamaran”, is 49 characters and keeps the part that distinguishes one booking from another. Also worth checking: 3.4 governs separators between inline items in a row, not subject lines, so nothing here is settled by it.
  • The booked name should be frozen at purchase. If {tourName} is composed at send time, correcting a title or a hub after a booking silently renames a product the traveller has already paid for, so the reminder and the cancellation disagree with the confirmation already in their inbox. Snapshot the composed name onto the booking record at checkout and compose only as a fallback.
  • Suppression is bound to a page, but a hub page carries cross-hub modules. “Also worth your time on Curaçao” and related hubs sit on the Klein Curaçao page. As written those tours would lose their label on a page whose H1 names a different place. Bind suppression to the module, not the page: any module whose set is guaranteed single-hub and is introduced by a heading naming that hub.
  • The colon form is an English concatenation. Seven locales. French needs a narrow space before a colon, Chinese uses the full-width colon, and Dutch and German name this product with a preposition rather than a colon. “Title Case, 35 to 60 characters” is an English rule: German capitalises by grammar, Chinese has neither case nor a comparable count. This needs a per-locale format, not one hardcoded string, and primary_hub needs to be a reference to a localised hub record rather than one English string.
  • Analytics. GA4 item_name is never specified. If it follows the surface, one product splits across several rows in revenue reporting. It should be one value platform-wide, and the composed name is the sensible one.
  • The eyebrow wears badge clothing. It is a bordered, shadowed pill in the CTA colour with a map pin, on cards that already carry Sponsored, Popular, New and Selling fast in the same shape. Worth deciding whether a place label should look like a commercial badge, and whether the pin, which conventionally means “where you are”, is right when the card also has a departure city. Its 1px border is 1.26:1 against white, so the border carries no meaning for anyone who cannot see it; the text at 4.58:1 does the work.
What to do, in order.
  • Write the 3.5 note and the conflict log entry for the hub-page deviation. Nothing else is safe until the spec and the decision agree.
  • Clean the stored titles with a hub-aware data pass, then delete the stripping logic from the surfaces that compensate for it today.
  • Let the hub page compose hub plus title, the listing surfaces add the eyebrow, and the tour page compose the colon form. The last breadcrumb crumb takes the tour name only.
  • Move the eyebrow out of the rating row into its own element above the title, in the build and in mck-04. Keep 3.5’s inline middot form available where space is tight.
  • Answer the open items before building anything after checkout, starting with the subject line and the frozen booked name.
