What changes
  • On mobile the search opens as one full-screen layer instead of a panel hanging under the bar, so the keyboard no longer eats the suggestions.
  • The whole pill goes into the layer, not just the query segment. What? and When? stay side by side, so a visitor who wants a date never has to close anything first, and there is never a second search bar behind the layer.
  • An X on the left of the pill closes the layer. Today the panel has no close control at all.
  • The body switches between the suggestions and the calendar, depending on which segment is active. The orange circle stays the search action, as in the hero.
  • All four groups stay reachable while typing: categories and hubs, collections, best selling trips, and the closing link to every tour.
  • Closing returns the visitor to the same spot on the page with anything they picked still filled in. The nav search icon on mobile should open the same layer, so there is one panel with two entry points.
  • Desktop is unchanged: the pill keeps its dropdown and the nav search stays the persistent entry.
