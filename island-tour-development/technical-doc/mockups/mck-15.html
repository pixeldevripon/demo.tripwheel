What moved, and why
  • The field stays "Travelers" with the count. The split by age band lives in the breakdown, where it belongs.
  • Three things about the state before anything is chosen, which the Reset link under the card shows: the extras row is already there but closed, the party already sits at the tour minimum, and the button still reads "Check availability" because there is no date yet.
  • The travelers panel opens and closes from its own header, like the extras block, and the price moves on every plus and minus. That drops the Apply button the brief asks for: it existed because the panel used to overlay the fields and needed an exit, and the counters already give live feedback on capacity, so holding back only the price was the odd half of it.
  • Optional extras sits in the card from the start, closed. Same treatment the brief gives the spectators field: in step 1, never expanded by default, never framed as an upsell. It also means only one block appears later instead of two, so the button moves less.
  • The infant line reads "1 infant · Free" rather than "Infants (0-3) x 1 x $0   $0". The brief already bans zero rows for bands nobody picked; a band that was picked should read like a fact, not like arithmetic about nothing.
  • The bands come out in a fixed order, adults, then children, then infants, both in the panel and in the breakdown. Add an infant in the build today and it drops in between adult and child.
  • Total, Pay today and Balance later are their own block again, not contents of the travelers box.
  • Extras sit above the price, so adding the open bar changes a number the visitor is still looking at.
  • Microcopy: "$150 per person" not "$150/per person", "2 adults × $150" not "Adult x 2 x $150", and the breakdown collapses with a word rather than a bare arrow.
  • The time chip is gone: this tour has one departure, and the picker only belongs on tours with more than one.
Bug in the build: a per-person extra is charged twice
  • With two adults, adding one open bar upgrade puts $44 on the total instead of $22. The party size is being applied on top of the number the visitor picked, so the same extra is counted twice.
  • The stepper counts units, and the unit is whatever the price line says. "$22 per person" means one step is one person, so one open bar is $22 no matter how big the party is. Two open bars for two adults is the visitor's choice to make, not ours to assume.
  • Cap the per-person stepper at the number of paying travelers, so nobody can buy five open bars for four people.
  • A per-booking extra like the GoPro package cannot be bought twice at all. Either cap that one at one, or give it a plain add and remove instead of a counter.
  • The live card above does this correctly: add one open bar with any party and the total moves by $22.

A line through the date means there was a departure and it can no longer be had: "Sold out" when the trip is full, "Closed" when the booking cutoff has passed. Plain grey with "No departure" means the trip does not run that day at all. Ring is today, orange outline is the first date that can be booked.
Wording, front and back
  • Three labels, not one: "Sold out", "No departure", "Closed". The line through the date carries a rule rather than a single state: there was a departure here and it can no longer be had. Sold out and past the cutoff both qualify; a day the trip never runs does not, so it stays plain grey. That is the distinction a visitor can still read on a phone, where hover does not exist.
  • In practice the two struck states rarely get confused even without hovering: cutoff dates always sit at the top of the month next to the past ones, sold-out dates sit further out.
  • "Closed" keeps the meaning it has in the spec, a date past the booking cutoff. It is not a catch-all.
  • "Sold out" and not "Fully booked": it is already the wording in the portal legend, on the time chips, and behind the "Likely to sell out" label.
  • A sold-out day should look the same whether it filled through us or the operator marked it in the portal. That needs the reason field on the portal close.
  • Both "N left" signals are parked at the founder's call, the one on dates and the one on the chips. v1 ships time-only chips plus "Sold out"; the locked brief version is shown alongside as a later option.

  • One word per idea: "Sold out" in the portal legend, in the reason button, on the state pill, on the traveler calendar, on the time chips, and behind the "Likely to sell out" label. Not "Fully booked" anywhere.
  • The July 29 review already put "Add one-off departure" against the no-departure state, and already had a reason row on the close, worded "Fully booked · Other". This narrows it to two buttons and renames the first, so the reason and the state finally use the same word.
  • Every close ends with a confirmation that names the consequence and offers Undo: "New sales stopped. 52 booked guests keep their bookings." That guest count is the reassurance an operator needs before closing a full day, and the review already writes it.
  • A day marked sold out while the count reads low is the normal case, not a bug, because the count only sees Island Tours bookings. The row says so in place rather than leaving the operator to work it out from "Sold out · 12/70".
  • A one-off departure carries a dashed outline, so a month later it is still obvious what recurs from the weekly schedule and what was added for one date only.
  • Today is ringed, past days fade and cannot be changed. History stays readable, but nothing can be closed after the fact.
  • The reason question has a way out. Tapping Close by accident should never force a choice.
  • The same two reasons apply to "Close / reopen a range" and both have to show in Date changes, so the register reads "Whole day closed · Sold out" rather than just "closed".
  • Everything pressable here is drawn as a shape in this file, but it has to ship as a real button, reachable by keyboard and at least a fingertip tall. Operators run this from a phone on a dock, often one-handed and often wet.
  • One rule for the whole day card, so an operator never has to guess: if it has a border you can press it, if it is plain text it only describes something. Solid fill is reserved for the one headline action of the day, an outline carries Close and Reopen per departure, and states like Open or Sold out stay plain text. Today Close sits next to Open as two identical-looking words, one a label and one a control.
  • Nothing new goes in the calendar cell. Every day opens the same way, by tapping it, and the actions live in the day card underneath. An empty cell only carries the quiet "No departures" hint the review already asks for.
  • It also fixes the sell-out count behind the demand card. That count only sees trips that fill through us, so a tour that sells out at the operator every week never qualifies. An operator-marked sold out counts; a Not running does not.
  • Worth reconciling in the same pass: the all-tours agenda preselects a Weather reason chip on "Close all of today". Weather is a Not running, so those two lists have to agree.
