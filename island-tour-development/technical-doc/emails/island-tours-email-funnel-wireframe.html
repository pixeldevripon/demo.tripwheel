Subject B (A/B): Open departures this week on Curaçao
Preview: Three with spots open this week. Already home? Save them for next time.
Trigger: tour_end plus 72h, Curaçao morning · marketing rail, soft opt-in, unsubscribe mandatory, tours only. Built as an availability email, not a recommendation email: in the 2025 benchmark set a generic cross-sell is the worst-performing automation there is (0.87% conversion, highest unsubscribe at 0.89%), while the availability archetype converts at 6.72% with a 0.37% unsubscribe. About 40% of tour bookings happen inside a two-day window, and that last-minute group is driven by flexibility (41%), with price dead last (15%), which is why this email carries a free-reschedule line and no discount. Three cards only, selected as contrast, adjacent, flagship; availability is pulled live at send time and a tour with no open departure in 7 days is dropped. No countdown, no "only 2 spots left". Full detail: island-tours-next-adventure-email-wireframe.html.
Subject: Your booking is cancelled
Preview: Refund on its way. No forms, no questions asked.
Trigger: the traveler taps Cancel booking, which opens a tokenized confirmation page (never a raw one-click cancel), the request goes to the admin, the admin processes the refund and confirms by email. The deadline is judged on the request timestamp, not the admin action. No wireframe has been built for this email; the copy below is the locked wording from master 6.4, and it is payment-model aware (paid_in_full opens with "Your payment is on its way back from us"; operator_full carries no refund line and reads "Nothing was paid to Island Tours. Already paid the operator? Then the operator refunds you directly."). Cancellation stops the reminder, the review request and the marketing rail.
