Build notes. Email-safe single-column layout (max 600px), inline styles in production, port to React Email for Resend. Brand orange #E8611A; SVG line icons in neutral gray #6B7280 per LD20 (no emoji in the body). Hero image has alt = tour name. Block 5 (How to pay the rest) and the anti-fraud line carry the C2 anti-phishing mitigation and must stay above the fold of the payment area, not buried in the footer. Cancel button is a tokenized link that opens a cancellation request form on island.tours, never a raw one-click cancel; submitting emails the request to the admin, who processes the refund and confirms by email. Conditionals: pickup vs meeting point, duration, special requests, what-to-bring all render or hide per booking. Currency and date are locale-formatted (USD for EN/ZH, EUR for NL/DE/FR/ES/PT); times are shown 24-hour across locales. Deliverability (SPF/DKIM/DMARC on a dedicated transactional subdomain) is the real lever on the mitigation; this wireframe shows copy and hierarchy only.
