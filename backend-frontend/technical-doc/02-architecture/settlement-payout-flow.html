Payment Settlement & Payout Flow

Island Tours - visual companion to SETTLEMENT-AND-PAYOUTS.md. How money moves between the traveler, Island Tours, and the operator, and how each side is settled.

The reframe: one target, three deviations

The platform's goal on every booking is to end up holding exactly its commission. Each model just collects a different amount at checkout relative to that target.

ModelCollected at checkoutvs. commission targetSettlement needed
Deposit models (operator_link, on_arrival)deposit (= commission by design)equalnone (self-settling)

Two classic marketplace problems: paid_in_full is the payout problem (platform holds money, owes the supplier net). operator_full is the commission-collection problem (supplier holds money, owes the platform its cut).

V1  Money flow now

Three live models. operator_full is dropped from v1. Deposit models self-settle; paid_in_full pays the operator out on a schedule after the cancellation window closes (clawback-safe).

Money to Island Tours (commission) Money to operator Not used in v1

1 + 2. Deposit models - operator_link and on_arrival

Self-settling. commission == deposit_pct, so no transfer occurs. The operator receives the rest of the payment directly. Ledger row is record-only: net_position ~ 0.

3. paid_in_full - scheduled payout after the cancellation window

Scheduled payout (clawback-safe). Island Tours takes 100%, retains its commission, and pays the remainder to the operator on a schedule after the cancellation window closes - so a refunded cancellation never lands after the operator was already paid. Ledger: net_position = + (total - commission), status = RECORDED until the window closes, then PAID_OUT.

Dropped in V1  operator_full

Removed in v1. Returns in v2 via Stripe Connect or direct bank transfer, when the commission-collection rail is specified.

V2  Merchant of record via Stripe Connect

Route every chargeable leg through the platform. The commission is skimmed as the Connect application fee; the operator is paid out automatically on a schedule. Every leg becomes machine-readable.

paid_in_full and both deposit models map to a single destination charge with application_fee_amount = commission. The settlements ledger is then populated from Stripe events instead of manual entry.

operator_full in v2 - two options.
  • Route via Connect (traveler pays through the platform). Makes it trackable and auto-collects commission, but it effectively becomes paid_in_full.
  • Direct bank transfer + commission invoice (operator collects, Island Tours invoices for commission via SEPA / card-on-file mandate, listing suspension on non-payment). Preserves the off-platform model but keeps collection risk and stays non-machine-readable.
paid_in_full payout - decided: it is a scheduled payout released after the cancellation window closes (clawback-safe), not an immediate in-flow payout. V1 runs this as a manual/batch payout against the ledger; v2 automates it via a Connect destination charge with the transfer released on the same post-window schedule.

The settlements ledger (build in v1, extend later)

Every booking writes exactly one settlement row at confirmation, regardless of model. net_position sign is fixed: positive = Island Tours owes the operator; negative = operator owes Island Tours.

The ledger is the extension point. Building it in v1 means phase 2 changes only the executor (Stripe Connect transfers, scheduled payouts, operator_full invoicing), never the data model. Settlement is separate from tracking: exactly one booking_complete still fires with booking_value = commission_amount in EUR, never GMV.

How the global leaders settle

PlatformModelHow the money settles
Viator (Merchant API), GetYourGuide, KlookMerchant of record (= paid_in_full)Traveler pays the OTA in full; OTA keeps commission and remits net to the operator on a schedule. Viator: monthly, ~21 business days after the travel month (weekly PayPal option). GYG: monthly default, bi-weekly for +2% commission.
AirbnbMerchant of record + fast payoutGuest pays Airbnb in full; host is paid out ~24h after check-in, net of fees, via Payoneer / bank / PayPal.
Booking.com (legacy)Pay-at-property (= operator_full)Guest pays the property directly; Booking.com invoices monthly for commission and collects by direct debit / bank transfer / virtual card. The model everyone is migrating away from because of collection leakage.
Direction of travel: every mature marketplace converges on merchant-of-record. It kills commission leakage and makes every transaction trackable - which is exactly why v2 targets Stripe Connect.
